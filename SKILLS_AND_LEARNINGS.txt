Project ID: 6
Title: A Large Language Model (LLM)
Category: AI Model
Language: Python
Source Tutorial URL: https://github.com/rasbt/LLMs-from-scratch

SKILLS USED:
- Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) subword tokenization and special-token splitting
- Reverse-mode automatic differentiation over dynamic computation graphs
- Multi-head causal self-attention with upper-triangular future masking
- Pre-LayerNorm GPT transformer block with GELU MLP and residual streams
- AdamW optimizer with bias-corrected momentum and decoupled weight decay
- Autoregressive decoding (greedy, temperature scaling, and top-k sampling)

WHAT I LEARNED MAKING THIS:
Building a GPT transformer without PyTorch makes the mechanics of causal attention concrete: every token position t builds query vectors that are strictly forbidden from attending to keys/values at s > t, enforcing autoregressive generation during parallel sequence training. Pre-LayerNorm stabilizes residual gradient flow across transformer layers, while BPE cleanly bridges raw character vocabularies and frequent words using deterministic merge rules.
